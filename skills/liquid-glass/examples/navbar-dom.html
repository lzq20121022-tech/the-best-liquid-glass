Glass over live content

DOM engine: real refraction through SVG backdrop-filter in Chromium browsers; Safari and Firefox get a tuned frosted fallback automatically. Scroll and watch the header, the tab bar and the round button.

Liquid Glass behaves like a thick convex lens: light is bent and concentrated at the rim while the flat center stays clear. Text passing under the edge of the header bends along the outline instead of simply blurring away.

The displacement map is baked per element from the same physics as the WebGL renderer: a squircle height profile across the bevel and Snell’s law at every pixel. Three displaced channels with slightly different strengths create the chromatic fringe.

Dark section

Glass that sits on dark content should switch to a dark appearance. In the DOM engine the tone comes from the page background, or you can pass tone: 'dark'.

Text, icons and controls inside the glass use color: var(--lg-fg), which the library sets per element to keep them legible.
